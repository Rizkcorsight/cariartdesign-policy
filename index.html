Updated September 19, 2026. Developer: Rizk Corsight, LLC. Contact: rizkcorsight@rizkcorsight.com. This update corrects the description of existing Android SDK technical data collection. The English policy controls if translations differ.

Photos and face processing

Caricature generation processes your selected photos and face landmarks on your device. These images, landmarks and generated results are not uploaded to Google or the developer for processing. You can export or share your artwork through services you choose. The app requires no sign-in and shows no advertisements.

Android technical data sent to Google

Android uses Google ML Kit for on-device face detection. Its SDK transmits the following information to Google: installation identifiers, device and app information, and technical usage, performance and error metrics. Google uses these for diagnostics, usage analytics, maintaining and improving its APIs, and detecting misuse. These metrics do not contain your photos or face-processing results. ML Kit sends this information over HTTPS. Google processes it under its own privacy policy; it is not limited to acting on our instructions.

Google Play Billing also sends technical SDK-operation and diagnostic information to Google, including app/device information, response and error details and a client correlation identifier. Store connections can occur automatically to check access, not only when you press Buy or Restore. These Android Google SDK disclosures do not describe the iOS version.

The app offers 3 days of full access, followed by an optional one-time unlock. Starting the trial locally creates no charge or subscription. Nothing automatically renews. Apple and Google platform services may contact their servers for purchases, access checks and art-model delivery. Model downloads do not upload your photos. Platform providers handle service information under their own privacy policies.

Storage, permissions and choices

Saved artwork and settings are stored on your device. Your own device backups may include them. Camera and photo permissions support taking, choosing or saving images; you can revoke permissions in device settings. Deleting artwork or uninstalling the app removes local app data, but not your exports, backups or records retained by platform providers. On Android, drawing images may also be cached locally to restore a session.

The app has no separate switch to disable Google SDK technical metrics. Google controls retention and privacy choices for information it receives; uninstalling does not promise deletion of its existing records.
